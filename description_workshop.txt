[h1]TerraWorld Blueprints[/h1]
Design a building in [url=https://terraworld.dev/structure-studio]TerraWorld Structure Studio[/url], export it as a [b].twpack[/b] file, and bring it into your world as a ghost blueprint. Build it by hand along the ghost, or feed the materials to a Blueprint Builder and watch it go up on its own.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Design or edit a structure in Structure Studio (free, runs in the browser) and export it as [b].twpack[/b].
[*]Put the file in [i]Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/TerraWorldBlueprints/[/i]. The tablet's [b]Open folder[/b] button takes you there.
[*]Craft a [b]Blueprint Tablet[/b] (10 Wood at a Work Bench). Right-click it to pick a blueprint, then left-click to project it. The cursor marks the bottom centre.
[*]Build by hand along the ghost, or craft a [b]Blueprint Builder[/b] (20 Wood, 5 Iron or Lead Bars and the tablet at an Anvil), link it to the projection and deposit the materials.
[/olist]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Ghost projection[/b]: blue cells still need building, red cells are blocked by something else, finished cells disappear. Projections are saved with the world.
[*][b]Hover a ghost cell[/b] to see the item it needs, with how many you carry against how many the visible part still needs. The line turns green once you have enough.
[*][b]Show or hide[/b] blocks, walls and liquids separately.
[*][b]Layer by layer[/b]: show one band of 1 to 12 rows at a time, from the bottom up. [b]Lowest unfinished[/b] jumps to the first band with work left, and PageUp/PageDown step through bands while you build.
[*][b]Blueprint Builder[/b]: shows every material with stored / needed counts and pulls what it needs from your inventory, Piggy Bank, Safe, Defender's Forge and Void Vault (favourited items are left alone), then builds once everything is paid for.
[*][b]Magic Storage support[/b] (optional): the Builder can also take materials from the nearest Magic Storage network. Magic Storage is not required.
[*][b]Open TerraWorld / Structure Studio[/b] straight from the tablet to design your next blueprint.
[*]Keeps slopes, half blocks, paint, Echo and Illuminant coatings, wires, actuators, furniture styles, sign text and chest names.
[/list]

[h2]Fair by default[/h2]
Every cell is paid for with the item that places it. Chest contents are never copied. Blocks already in the way are never destroyed. Tiles with no item (grass, pots, natural walls) are skipped unless the server config allows placing them for free. Liquids are off by default and cost one bucket per cell when enabled.

[h2]Limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]The mod must be installed on the server and on every client.
[*]The Blueprint Builder only works in single-player for now. In multiplayer the ghosts still work for building by hand.
[*]Blueprints that use tiles from other mods need those mods enabled. Their tile entities are not recreated.
[*]English text only.
[/list]
